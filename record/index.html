Tests

First run

Holds, within its domain: replays

One world of 32 cells, 128 steps. Can it be replayed exactly?

Yes. Events, measures and summary match byte for byte. One seed only, so it says nothing general.

Protocol
E0-v2
Design
One trajectory. N = 32, density 0.25, seed 183742, 128 synchronous steps.
Numbers
Occupancy 0.34375 to 0.1875. Resource total 32 at both ends. Events, metrics and summary match byte for byte.
Limit
One seed, no control.

Law 25%

Does the law do anything compared with a frozen world? Sparse start.

Fewer groups in all 64 pairs. Mean change −4.27 (95% interval −4.67 to −3.86).

Protocol
E0-law-ablation-v1
Design
64 matched pairs, seeds 183743 to 183806. N = 32, density 0.25, 128 steps, majority against a frozen control.
Numbers
Mean paired change in groups −4.265625, 95% t interval [−4.671690, −3.859560]. Negative in 64 of 64.
Limit
One law, one size, one density.

Minimal world

All at once?

Holds, within its domain: 49/64

Does it matter if cells update together or one by one?

Yes. 49 of 64 matched starts end elsewhere (95% interval 65% to 85%).

Protocol
E0-update-schedule-v1
Design
The 64 starts of the previous test, synchronous against one random sequential order each.
Numbers
49 of 64 endings differ, 0.765625, Wilson 95% [0.648667, 0.852501].
Limit
One order per start.
Bears on
Minimal world

Four orders

Holds, within its domain: 56/64

Same start, four random orders. One ending or several?

Several for 56 of 64 starts. 2.67 endings on average, a lower bound.

Protocol
E0-sequential-order-replicates-v1
Design
The same 64 starts, four independent random orders each, order seeds 910000 to 910255.
Numbers
56 of 64 starts reach several endings, 0.875, Wilson 95% [0.772254, 0.935278]. Mean 2.671875 endings.
Limit
Four orders give a lower bound.

Minimal world

One poke

Holds, within its domain: 35/64

Flip one cell of a settled world. Does it come back?

35 of 64 end elsewhere, 29 are restored exactly. The link with borders was noticed after the fact, so it was retested.

Protocol
E0-single-flip-response-v1
Design
64 settled worlds, one flip at tick 8, target = seed mod 32.
Numbers
35 of 64 endings change, 0.546875, Wilson 95% [0.425736, 0.662706]. 29 return exactly.
Limit
The link with borders was seen afterwards, then retested.
Bears on
Persistence & response

Poke 32 cells

Holds, within its domain: 64·64·0

Registered in advance: does the distance to a border decide?

At distance 0 and 1, all 64 change. At distance 2 or more, none does.

Protocol
E0-interface-distance-response-v1
Design
64 settled worlds at N = 32, three flips each, at border distance 0, 1 and 2 or more. Registered in advance.
Numbers
64/64, 64/64 and 0/64 endings change. Wilson 95% [0.9434, 1] near, [0, 0.0566] far.
Limit
Lowest eligible cell only. One law, one density.
Bears on

64 new worlds at N = 64, seeds 183871 to 183934, three flips each.

Persistence & response, Scale

Two-bit loop

Toy model only: 8 cases

In a tiny loop, does cutting an inner link stop what it maintains?

Yes, at step 9, and replacing the role keeps it. But we wrote the rule, so this is a check of the fixture.

Protocol
ORG-01-counterfactual-closure-v2
Design
Two-bit deterministic loop, eight conditions in two orientations, 64 transitions, cut between ticks 8 and 15.
Numbers
Output lost at state 9 under the cut; kept under sham, inner substitution and outside replacement. 16 exact replays.
Limit
The transition rule encodes the contrast. Not run on the Otto engine.
Bears on
Organisation

Renewal measure

Rejected: 38/128

Can a measure tell real self-renewal from look-alikes, whoever is looking?

All look-alikes were caught. But 38 of 128 paths changed verdict with the observer, so we rejected our own measure.

Protocol
G02-MEASUREMENT-V1
Design
327 paths: 128 candidate, 128 paired external mirrors, 64 noise, 7 deterministic. Eight admissible observers, locked before the run.
Numbers
Positive reference passes 8 of 8 observers. 198 negative paths: no pass. 38 of 128 candidate paths change verdict across observers.
Limit
Rejected as observer-sensitive. Says nothing on life, autonomy or organisation.
Bears on

Objectives

Minimal world

Measured, within a bounded domain

Local transitions on a finite state, visible and replayable.

How we would test it
Run the active law and a frozen copy from identical starts. Replay every event.
What could fool us
Taking a replay for an independent replication; Complexity that the law itself supplied
On record
Characterised for one law, majority on a ring. Not closed.
May lead to
Persistence & response (supported), Resources (plausible), Boundary (plausible), Causal memory (plausible), Heredity (plausible)

Scale

Not started, no evidence yet

How effects, costs and limits move with size, resources and time.

How we would test it
Sweep size and capacity separately, then together, against noise and periodic controls.
What could fool us
Novelty from noise; A finite curve read as unlimited growth
On record
One size change tested, 32 to 64 cells. Nothing more.
May lead to
Self-change (optional) (contested or unknown), Otto speaks and understands by itself (plausible)

Persistence & response

Measured, within a bounded domain

A pattern lasts over a declared time and responds to a declared disturbance.

How we would test it
Flip one cell, compare with a sham and with a plain attractor.
What could fool us
A crystal; A standing wave; An oscillator; A static attractor
On record
Response to single pokes is mapped at 32 and 64 cells. Not closed.
May lead to
Organisation (contested or unknown), Otto speaks and understands by itself (refuted)

Boundary

We are working here

A unit keeps its causal identity while its parts are replaced.

How we would test it
Move the proposed boundary and cut interactions of equal cost. See if predictions hold.
What could fool us
A boundary drawn after seeing the result; Plain spatial grouping
On record
No evidence yet.
May lead to
Organisation (plausible)

Resources

We are working here

Limited flows change what can happen and what can be maintained.

How we would test it
Withdraw one flow against a sham, with the same starting stock.
What could fool us
An energy counter that never moves; A budget that encodes the answer
On record
E0 only keeps a constant count. No flow measured yet.
May lead to
Organisation (plausible)

Causal memory

Not started, no evidence yet

A trace of the past changes a later response.

How we would test it
Two histories, same present. Erase or transplant the trace and nothing else.
What could fool us
Autocorrelation; An unchanged environment; An attractor taken for learning
On record
No evidence yet.
May lead to
Plasticity (plausible), Adaptation (plausible), Communication (plausible), Seeking information (plausible)

Plasticity

Not started, no evidence yet

Interaction changes a rule or a link, and the change lasts.

How we would test it
Lock the structure or let it move, then test in a reserved context.
What could fool us
A law picked from outside; A new state under a fixed law
On record
No evidence yet.
May lead to
Adaptation (plausible)

Organisation

We are working here

Activities help maintain the very conditions that make them possible.

How we would test it
First a measurement that rejects trivial look-alikes. Then cut, replace and compensate inner roles.
What could fool us
A flame; Convection; A simple feedback loop; A criterion fitted to the model
On record
A toy model behaved as written. The first real measurement was rejected. This is where we work.
May lead to
Adaptation (contested or unknown)

Heredity

Not started, no evidence yet

A variation survives the replacement of the units that carry it.

How we would test it
Remove the transmitted channel, keep the same surroundings.
What could fool us
The environment rebuilding the same thing; A copy made by the program
On record
No evidence yet.
May lead to
Evolution (part of the definition)

Seeking information

Not started, no evidence yet

An action changes what can be seen, and that changes a later decision.

How we would test it
Offer probes of equal cost and scramble which one is informative.
What could fool us
Random exploration; Information coded as a reward
On record
No evidence yet.
May lead to
Asking (plausible), Its own research (plausible)

Adaptation

Not started, no evidence yet

The response to a context changes after interaction, with a bounded causal benefit.

How we would test it
Reverse a regularity. Compare trace transfer, erasure and plain selection.
What could fool us
Passive sorting of survivors; A curriculum we supplied; A good fixed controller
On record
No evidence yet.
May lead to
Evolution (contested or unknown)

Evolution

Not started, no evidence yet

Transmitted variants change in frequency because they spread differently.

How we would test it
Intact against scrambled inheritance, unequal against equalised spread.
What could fool us
Drift; Sorting without heredity; A hidden evolutionary goal
On record
No evidence yet.
May lead to
New individuals (contested or unknown), Accumulation (contested or unknown)

Communication

Not started, no evidence yet

An emission changes what another system does next. Not words yet: a signal that matters.

How we would test it
Scramble the channel, keep shared inputs, replace the sender with a matched replay.
What could fool us
Correlation through a shared input; A message written by an outside agent
On record
No evidence yet.
May lead to
Accumulation (plausible), Asking (contested or unknown)

Accumulation

Not started, no evidence yet

A lineage keeps and combines changes beyond what it would rediscover alone.

How we would test it
Chains with an intact or scrambled channel, against an isolated control with equal budget.
What could fool us
More compute; The same discovery made twice; Random complexity
On record
No evidence yet.
May lead to
Asking (contested or unknown), Otto speaks and understands by itself (part of the definition)

New individuals

Not started, no evidence yet

A group starts to transmit and depend on itself at its own level.

How we would test it
Scatter and rebuild groups from the same parts.
What could fool us
Aggregation; Temporary cooperation; A boundary drawn by the observer
On record
No evidence yet.
May lead to
Accumulation (contested or unknown)

Asking

Not started, no evidence yet

A distinction between possibilities decides which experiment comes next.

How we would test it
Swap what two probes would reveal, then cut the answer. See if choices follow.
What could fool us
A preprogrammed list of questions; A spoken request with no consequence
On record
No evidence yet.
May lead to
Causal prediction (plausible)

Causal prediction

Not started, no evidence yet

A retained structure predicts what an unseen intervention will do.

How we would test it
Reserve interventions and recombinations. Ablate the candidate relation.
What could fool us
Interpolation; A lookup table; A model that lives in the observer
On record
No evidence yet.
May lead to
Accumulation (contested or unknown), Understanding (plausible)

Understanding

Not started, no evidence yet

A relation keeps working after its surface, its place or its context has changed.

How we would test it
Change labels, topology and cause one by one. Compare intact and scrambled carriers.
What could fool us
The same data in disguise; An interface that encodes the mapping
On record
No evidence yet.
May lead to
Its own research (plausible), Otto speaks and understands by itself (part of the definition)

Its own research

Not started, no evidence yet

Otto proposes, intervenes, reads the result and revises, with nobody choosing for it.

How we would test it
A hidden reserved world. Falsify one relation and check that the next test changes.
What could fool us
A fixed lab script; An outside agent thinking for Otto
On record
No evidence yet.
May lead to
Self-change (optional) (contested or unknown), Otto speaks and understands by itself (part of the definition)

Self-change (optional)

Not started, no evidence yet

A change Otto makes improves its ability to make further useful changes.

How we would test it
Freeze the mechanism after one cycle and compare on a reserved problem.
What could fool us
An outside optimiser; A one-off gain; Overfitting the measure
On record
An optional branch. No evidence yet.
May lead to
Otto speaks and understands by itself (contested or unknown)

Otto speaks and understands by itself

Not started, no evidence yet

Autonomous, cumulative intelligence: gains in inquiry and transfer, kept and reused across contexts.

How we would test it
Combine separate audits of revision, retention and transfer, each with its own ablations.
What could fool us
A single good score; A supplied library of answers; Complexity with no function
On record
The goal. No current result reaches it.
