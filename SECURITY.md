# Security

Found a security problem on the Otto website? Please write to ottoalife@gmail.com, in English or French, with the page and the steps. Good-faith reports are welcome.

Please do not open a public issue before it is fixed, do not load the site heavily, and do not touch other people's data.

Contact file: https://maliciousmusic.github.io/otto-site/.well-known/security.txt
