The film follows the site’s rule: each result keeps its limit next to it. The map of possible answers and the proposed path are design documents, not results. Keyboard: space to play or pause, arrows to move between lines.

French narration synthesised with Piper and the “siwis” voice, trained on the SIWIS French Speech Synthesis Database (CC BY 4.0, University of Edinburgh). English captions are read by your browser’s voice when it has one.
