How a face fits in a letter

The page holds ordinary letters. Search engines, AI readers, screen readers and copy-paste all see words, because the faces live inside the font file.

The font itself passes the letters around between a smiler, a wonderer and a winker, so neighbours never blink together.

Nothing moves around it

Letters keep their width whatever their mood, and the faces step aside at small sizes or when you ask for less motion.
