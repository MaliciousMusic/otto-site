Accessibility

Last updated: 24 November 2026.

We want everyone to be able to read and play here. Our target is WCAG 2.2 level AA. We checked every page with an automated tool (axe) and tested keyboard use and narrow screens. Screen readers have not been tested yet. The site has not been audited by an independent body, so we do not claim conformance.

What is in place

  • Everything works with a keyboard. In the ring, the map and the world picker, arrow keys move and Enter acts.
  • The map has a list view, and charts have a table or a written reading.
  • Colour is never alone: faces, shapes or words carry the same meaning.
  • Motion can be paused from the Display menu, and the site follows your system’s reduced-motion setting.
  • Letters with faces can be replaced by plain letters from the Display menu.
  • Pages can be read without JavaScript, except for the simulations.
  • Text stays readable at 400% zoom.

Known limits

  • The map as a drawing is dense on small screens. The list view holds the same content.
  • The space and time picture in the lab is an image. Its state is described in words below it and can be saved as a table.
  • The logo green and blue are light. Where they carry information we use darker versions, words or shapes.
  • The site is in English. Legal information is also in French.

Tell us

If something blocks you, write to ottoalife@gmail.com with the page and what happened. We will answer and offer the content in another form.

If you are in France and receive no satisfactory answer, you can contact the Défenseur des droits.
