Holds, within its domain

The rule really does something

64 worlds, each run twice from the same start: once frozen, once alive. With the rule on, scattered cells gather into fewer groups.

Limit. It does not show self-organisation in general. Majority rules are well known, and this is our calibration.

E0-law-ablation-v1

Design
64 matched pairs, seeds 183743 to 183806. N = 32, density 0.25, 128 steps, majority against a frozen control.
Numbers
Mean paired change in groups −4.265625, 95% t interval [−4.671690, −3.859560]. Negative in 64 of 64.
Limit
One law, one size, one density.

Holds, within its domain

Order alone can change the ending

Take one start and update its cells one by one, in four different random orders. 56 of 64 worlds end in more than one place. Pick a world to see its four endings.

Limit. Four orders only give a lower bound. Nothing here adapts.

E0-update-schedule-v1

Design
The 64 starts of the previous test, synchronous against one random sequential order each.
Numbers
49 of 64 endings differ, 0.765625, Wilson 95% [0.648667, 0.852501].
Limit
One order per start.

E0-sequential-order-replicates-v1

Design
The same 64 starts, four independent random orders each, order seeds 910000 to 910255.
Numbers
56 of 64 starts reach several endings, 0.875, Wilson 95% [0.772254, 0.935278]. Mean 2.671875 endings.
Limit
Four orders give a lower bound.

Holds, within its domain

Poke it deep and it heals

Flip one cell of a settled world. Near a border between groups, the world moves for good. Two cells away or more, it repairs itself exactly, at both sizes we tried.

Limit. A direct consequence of this rule. It is not resilience of something alive.

E0-single-flip-response-v1

Design
64 settled worlds, one flip at tick 8, target = seed mod 32.
Numbers
35 of 64 endings change, 0.546875, Wilson 95% [0.425736, 0.662706]. 29 return exactly.
Limit
The link with borders was seen afterwards, then retested.

E0-interface-distance-response-v1

Design
64 settled worlds at N = 32, three flips each, at border distance 0, 1 and 2 or more. Registered in advance.
Numbers
64/64, 64/64 and 0/64 endings change. Wilson 95% [0.9434, 1] near, [0, 0.0566] far.
Limit
Lowest eligible cell only. One law, one density.

E0-interface-distance-size-v1

Design
64 new worlds at N = 64, seeds 183871 to 183934, three flips each.
Numbers
64/64, 64/64 and 0/64 endings change. Matched near-minus-far contrast 1.000 in all 64.
Limit
One size transfer.

Smiles healed. Round mouths moved for good.

Toy model only

Cut a loop and it stops

Two bits that keep each other on. Cut one link between states 8 and 16 and the activity is lost at state 9. Give the role to a stand-in and it survives.

Limit. We wrote the rule, so the contrast was built in. A check of our tools, and no evidence of organisation.

ORG-01-counterfactual-closure-v2

Design
Two-bit deterministic loop, eight conditions in two orientations, 64 transitions, cut between ticks 8 and 15.
Numbers
Output lost at state 9 under the cut; kept under sham, inner substitution and outside replacement. 16 exact replays.
Limit
The transition rule encodes the contrast. Not run on the Otto engine.

Rejected

The day we rejected our own measure

We built a measure of self-renewal and locked its rules before running it. It caught all 198 look-alike paths. Then 38 of 128 candidate paths changed verdict depending on when and how we looked.

A fact about Otto should not depend on our glasses. So we dropped it, and the next test stays blocked.

G02-MEASUREMENT-V1

Design
327 paths: 128 candidate, 128 paired external mirrors, 64 noise, 7 deterministic. Eight admissible observers, locked before the run.
Numbers
Positive reference passes 8 of 8 observers. 198 negative paths: no pass. 38 of 128 candidate paths change verdict across observers.
Limit
Rejected as observer-sensitive. Says nothing on life, autonomy or organisation.

Dark ring: this path changed its verdict. Smile: counted as self-renewing.
